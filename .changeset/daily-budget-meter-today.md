**Today shows the daily budget next to the other spend KPIs.** When a daily budget is set, the Today KPI strip adds a daily budget tile with the day's estimated spend, the cap, and the percent used. The tile turns amber at 50 percent of the cap and red at 100 percent, and it says "over budget" once the cap is passed. With no daily budget set, the strip is unchanged.
