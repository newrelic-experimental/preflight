**Dollar figures on Today and Sessions are marked as estimates.** A small "est." marker sits beside the spend-today figure, the Sessions workflow spend total, and each session's cost. Hovering the marker on the two totals lists why the number can differ from a bill: list prices unless a rate multiplier, data residency premium, or custom pricing file is configured, cost estimated from the size of tool inputs and outputs before a session's first token report, coverage limited to this machine and the platforms Preflight hooks into, cache writes priced as ordinary input on Copilot in VS Code, and invoices that can post later and use their own day and time zone.
