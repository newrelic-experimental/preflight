Today now counts a session you resumed from an earlier day in Spend today and the activity heatmap, using only its activity from today. A session that only reported tokens no longer has its saved copy added on top of the live totals on Model Usage, Quality, Tool Selection and Cost per Tool. The Concurrent sessions chart on Today stops growing past 8 rows and scales its blocks instead, and its tooltips still show the real session count.
