**Daily and weekly spend baselines count what a resumed session spent inside the window.** The baselines behind the daily and weekly budget alerts, the budget status, and the dashboard's today total left out a session resumed from an earlier week, because its file is named for the day it started. Preflight now also reads session files changed in the last seven days. For such a session the weekly baseline adds the days of its per-day cost record that fall inside the window, and the daily baseline takes today's entry. A session that started inside the window still counts all its cost for the week. Any session with a per-day cost record now gives the daily baseline today's entry instead of a share worked out from its tool calls. A session that started before the window and has no per-day cost record counts nothing, because its spend cannot be placed on a day. A session with no per-day cost record and no tool calls, subagent cost or timeline also counts nothing for the day, the same as the dashboard's Spend Today. With resumed sessions on disk the baselines can rise a lot, so a budget alert can fire that did not before.
