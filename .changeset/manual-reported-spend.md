**Org-reported spend, entered by hand.** Settings has a Reported spend section where you type the figure your organization reports for today or this week. Preflight stamps the time it was saved, shows it in the daily budget tile on Today beside the estimate, and greys it with a stale label once its day has passed. Clearing it removes it. The value never changes Preflight's estimate or its budget alerts. A weekly value is kept and shown in Settings only.
