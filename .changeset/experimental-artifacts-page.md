**Experimental: browse the HTML reports your agent writes, grouped by repo.** With `experimentalArtifacts` on, the local dashboard gains an Artifacts page in the sidebar. It lists each indexed report under its repo or folder, and opening one shows it in a sandboxed origin. With the flag off, the sidebar entry is hidden and the page explains how to turn it on.
