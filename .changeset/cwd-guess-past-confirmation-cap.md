**An engine whose session guess stays unconfirmed for two minutes keeps the cost it drained for that session before the guess.** A Claude Code engine that took its session from the working directory's breadcrumb holds back its saves while it waits for its parent's breadcrumb to confirm the guess. After two minutes it stops holding back and saves the session's file as its own, without first counting what it drained for that session before the guess. Its saves merged its own totals into that file by keeping the larger of each, so a token report saved before the guess and one drained after it saved one report's tokens instead of two. A subagent turn it drained for that session before the guess was not saved at all. At the two-minute mark the engine now adds what it drained for the session before the guess to its own totals, so its saves, its exit save, its cost tools and its budget include it once. It adds none of the file's other cost, which may belong to another session, unless the parent's breadcrumb confirms the guess later. On native Windows the parent's breadcrumb never confirms a guess, so this is the usual path there.
