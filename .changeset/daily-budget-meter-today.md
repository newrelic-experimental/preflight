**Today shows the daily budget next to the other spend KPIs.** When a daily budget is set, the Today KPI strip adds a daily budget tile. It shows the same total as the spend today tile, the cap, and the percent used, rounded down. The tile turns amber at 50 percent of the cap and red at 100 percent, and it says "over budget" once the cap is passed. With no daily budget set, the strip is unchanged.
